About

Dr. Luna KC is an Assistant Professor in Global and International Studies in the Faculty of Indigenous Studies, Social Sciences and Humanities at the University of Northern British Columbia (UNBC). Her interdisciplinary research sits at the intersection of gender, armed conflict, Women, Peace and Security (WPS), cybersecurity, technology-facilitated gender-based violence, and disasters.

Before joining UNBC, she was a Postdoctoral Researcher at McGill University and taught at the School of Public Policy and Global Affairs at the University of British Columbia. She earned her PhD in 2019 and MA in 2011 in International Development Studies from Wageningen University in the Netherlands, with a focus on conflict, security, disaster, and public policy.

Her work draws on feminist security studies and international development, with regional expertise spanning South Asia, Southeast Asia, and North America. She has worked with and advised organizations including Plan International, the Canadian Research Institute for the Advancement of Women, Women Transforming Cities, National Defence Canada, and the Asian Development Bank. She is also a research co-lead with the Research Network on Women, Peace and Security.

At UNBC, Dr. KC supervises graduate students in International Studies and Gender Studies and is currently accepting graduate students.
