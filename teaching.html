Teaching

My teaching brings together critical, feminist, and interdisciplinary approaches to global politics, peace and security, technology, and crisis. I aim to connect theory with contemporary policy questions and everyday experiences of security and inequality.

Courses at UNBC

  • Cybersecurity: From Critical Perspectives
  • Gender, Peace and Security
  • Gender and Disasters
  • Gender and Global Crisis
  • Contemporary Feminist Theories

Teaching and Mentorship

My teaching interests include critical technology and cybersecurity, Women, Peace and Security, feminist security studies, conflict and post-conflict transformation, and the gendered dimensions of disasters and global crises. I also mentor graduate students working across International Studies and Gender Studies.

In 2025, I presented on critical technology, cybersecurity, and Women, Peace and Security in classroom settings at the Research Network on Women, Peace and Security’s Pedagogies for Peace Symposium.
