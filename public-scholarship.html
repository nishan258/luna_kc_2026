Public Scholarship

Alongside academic research, I contribute public-facing analysis on gender, peace and security, cybersecurity, political change, and developments in South Asia. This work connects scholarly research with current policy and public debates.

Selected Public Writing

  • “Gen Z protests brought about change in Nepal via the powers and perils of social media.” The Conversation, September 2025.
  • With Crystal Whetstone. “War, peace, and security: The pandemic’s impact on women and girls in Nepal and Sri Lanka.” The Conversation, August 2022.
  • With Priscyll A. Avoine. “Anniversary of peace deals in Nepal and Colombia: Views on female ex-soldiers need to be challenged.” The Conversation, November 2021.
  • “A call for feminist analysis in cybersecurity: highlighting the relevance of the Women, Peace, and Security agenda.” London School of Economics, 2021.
  • “The Rise of the Alt-Right in Canada: A feminist analysis.” Canadian Research Institute for the Advancement of Women, 2021.

Knowledge Mobilization

My public scholarship also includes policy-oriented work and research resources on Women, Peace and Security, cybersecurity, defence, and conflict prevention. I am available to the media as a subject-matter expert through UNBC.
