LUNA KC

Assistant Professor at University of Northern British Columbia

Researching gender, security and global change

Dr. Luna KC is an Assistant Professor in Global and International Studies at UNBC. Her interdisciplinary work examines gender, armed conflict, Women, Peace and Security, cybersecurity, technology-facilitated violence, postwar transformation, and disasters, with regional expertise in South Asia, Southeast Asia, and North America.
